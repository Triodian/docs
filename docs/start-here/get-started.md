Every app has a worked example that runs in your browser. Nothing in it is saved. Each example uses a fictional company, with every step filled in and every output ready to open in Word. It is the quickest way to see what an engagement produces.

| App | Worked example | What to look at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Board Direction | Harrow Valley Foods, an unregulated company, in standalone mode | Steps B0 to B9; the reality note at B1; the AI risk appetite statement and board minute at B9 |
| AI Risk Appetite, AI Use Assessment, AI Board Direction (connected) | Coastline Super, a fictional superannuation trustee, through the full regulated chain | Board directions BD1 to BD7; check K11 on each decision; the register in Lite scope; the APRA letter evidence index |
| Confidentiality Exposure Assessment | A deal matter | The profile P1 to P9, channels and rungs, counsel's field 24 |
| The US chain | Harlow Ridge Bank and Ashgrove Mutual Insurance Company, fictional US entities, through all 3 apps. Quillfield Securities, Fernhollow Health and Ridgeline Power & Transmission in AI Board Direction | The US sector packs; the ISO/IEC 42001 evidence index |

1. Open the app from the app switcher on any Triodian site and choose **Practice**. You can also use the worked example link on the sign-in page.
2. Go through the steps in order. Each screen tells you who proposes, who records, what the app will not allow and why.
3. Open an output. Every output shows:
   - its status (it stays a draft until the chair records the resolution)
   - the version it came from
   - the evidence line that maps it to the APRA letter, Prudential Standard CPS 230 and ISO/IEC 42001.

Nothing you do in practice mode is sent to a server.

When you are ready to run a real engagement, go to [Choose your path](./paths/index.md).
