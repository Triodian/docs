There are 4 paths through this documentation, one for each type of user. Each path tells you what you will do with the apps, which pages to read and in what order.

Most people follow one path. Some follow two. For example, a fractional general counsel (GC) who also installs the apps for clients reads both the Practitioner and Operator paths.

| You are | Path |
| --- | --- |
| A fractional GC, board adviser, external counsel or consultant who runs engagements for clients | [Practitioner](./practitioner.md) |
| A chair, director, chief executive officer (CEO), chief risk officer (CRO), chief information officer (CIO) or member of an AI governance team taking part in an engagement someone else runs | [Board member or executive](./board.md) |
| The person who installs and runs the apps, on your own Cloudflare account or on a practitioner's behalf | [Operator](./operator.md) |
| Someone who wants to change the code, the content or the Atlas records | [Contributor](./contributor.md) |

Not sure which path is yours? Run a [worked example](../get-started.md) first. It takes 10 minutes and shows what an engagement produces. After that, most people know which path fits them.
