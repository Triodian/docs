Four kinds of people use the suite, and they arrive with different questions. Each path below says what you will do with the apps, which pages to read, and in what order. Most people fit one; a fractional GC who also installs the apps for clients reads two.

| You are | Path |
| --- | --- |
| A fractional GC, board adviser, external counsel or consultant who runs engagements for clients | [Practitioner](./practitioner.md) |
| A chair, director, CEO, CRO, CIO or member of an AI governance team taking part in an engagement someone else runs | [Board member or executive](./board.md) |
| The person who installs and runs the apps, on your own Cloudflare account or on a practitioner's behalf | [Operator](./operator.md) |
| Someone who wants to change the code, the content or the Atlas records | [Contributor](./contributor.md) |

Not sure? Run a [worked example](../get-started.md) first: it takes ten minutes and shows what an engagement produces, which usually settles which path you are on.
