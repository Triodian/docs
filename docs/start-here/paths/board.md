You take part in an engagement someone else facilitates. The app tells you at each step what is yours to do: a director completes the B0 briefing and proposes positions; the chair alone records the resolution; the CEO or the technology owner confirms the inventory; the GC alone answers legal questions. The concept pages on roles and modes explain why the app refuses some actions, and the frameworks section holds the method in full.
