The code, the framework text and the Atlas records are licensed under Apache 2.0. The Triodian name and logo are trademarks. The licence does not cover them.

The full terms are on [triodian.com/licensing](https://triodian.com/licensing#app-suite). That page sets out what is free and what Triodian charges for. If this page and that page differ, that page is correct.

**Free, self-hosted:** the 5 apps, the installer, the suite test, the Metamodel and exchange schemas, the published Atlas records and the keyless tier of the Atlas MCP server.

**What Triodian charges for:** running (facilitating) engagements, practitioner deployments that Triodian manages, and paid support for organisations that host the apps themselves.

**Free community support:** raise a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Triodian) on the relevant repository, or use Discussions on the Obligations Atlas. We answer when we can.

**Support the project:** [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Triodian).

Report security problems privately. Do not raise them as issues. See [Security reporting](../contribute/security-reporting.md).
