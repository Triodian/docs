The code, the framework text and the Atlas records are licensed under Apache 2.0. The Triodian name and logo are trademarks and are not covered by that licence. The full terms, what is free and what Triodian charges for, are on [triodian.com/licensing](https://triodian.com/licensing#app-suite); that page is the source and this one only points to it.

**Free, self-hosted:** the five apps, the installer, the suite test, the Metamodel and exchange schemas, the published Atlas records and the keyless tier of the Atlas MCP server.

**What Triodian charges for:** facilitation of engagements, practitioner deployments administered by Triodian, and a paid support agreement for self-hosters.

**Community support, free and best effort:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Triodian) on each repository and Discussions on the Obligations Atlas.

**Support the project:** [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Triodian).

Security problems go through private reporting, not issues: see [Security reporting](../contribute/security-reporting.md).
