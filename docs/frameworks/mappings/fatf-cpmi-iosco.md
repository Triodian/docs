The FATF Recommendations, including Recommendation 15 (new technologies) and Recommendation 16 (payment transparency, revised 2025), and the CPMI-IOSCO *Principles for financial market infrastructures* (2012, for systemically important payment systems) are the payments baseline's sources, with the FSB third-party toolkit. No international standard setter has issued AI-specific guidance for payment providers; the baseline says so, and payments has no US overlay for the same reason.

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Areas | BP-PAYMENTS: onboarding and identity, transaction processing, fraud, sanctions and AML screening, disputes and chargebacks, customer service, treasury and liquidity |
| Critical operations | Payment processing; settlement; sanctions screening |
| Screening and fraud models | Failure cases: false positives stopping legitimate payments at scale; a sanctions hit missed after tuning for fewer alerts; a B3 candidate of thresholds changed without a person's sign-off |
| Payment agents | Authority A4; agent controls BC1; the B3 candidate of agents initiating payments without a value limit and a stop |
