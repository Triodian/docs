The International Association of Insurance Supervisors' *Application Paper on the supervision of artificial intelligence* (2 July 2025) applies the Insurance Core Principles to AI under five topics: risk-based supervision and proportionality; governance and accountability; robustness, safety and security; transparency and explainability; fairness, ethics and redress. It changes no ICP.

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Areas of an insurer | BP-INSURANCE: distribution, underwriting, pricing, claims, customer service, reinsurance and capital, investments, technology; the general insurer pack in Australia and the NAIC-based US pack build on it |
| Critical operations | Claims payment; policy issue and renewal; premium collection |
| Explainability and redress | Failure cases: a third-party model's output that cannot be explained to a claimant; valid claims declined automatically; a group excluded by granular pricing |
| Governance and accountability | The authority scale on claims triage (A2) and straight-through claims (A3); the human-checkpoint control BC1 |
| B3 candidates | Claim denial with no person reviewing it; pricing on data the customer cannot see; health or genetic data in models outside the insurer's control |
