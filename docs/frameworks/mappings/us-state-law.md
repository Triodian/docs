Three state laws apply across all five US sectors and are recorded in the Atlas under the United States with the state in the record's title.


> **Status.** The US packs are built and in review; worked examples and the installer's US option follow, and US counsel reviews the mapping before any US entity uses the apps. See [United States](/concepts/united-states).

## The instruments

| Instrument | Tier | What it reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| California CPPA regulations on automated decision-making technology, risk assessments and cybersecurity audits | Binding | ADMT duties from 1 January 2027; existing processing assessed by 31 December 2027; first submissions 1 April 2028 |
| Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA), from 1 January 2026 | Binding | Prohibited intentional uses; disclosure; Attorney General enforcement with a cure period |
| Colorado SB 26-189 (signed 14 May 2026, from 1 January 2027) | Binding from 2027 | Replaced the 2024 Colorado AI Act's risk-management and impact-assessment duties with ADMT disclosure and transparency |
