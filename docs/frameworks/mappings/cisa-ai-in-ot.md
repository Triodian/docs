*Principles for the Secure Integration of Artificial Intelligence in Operational Technology* (December 2025, by CISA with Australia's ACSC and six other national cyber agencies), *Adapting Zero Trust Principles to Operational Technology* (29 April 2026, CISA, DOE, FBI, State and DoD) and the IEC 62443 series on industrial automation and control systems security are the sources for both critical infrastructure baselines. The NIST critical infrastructure profile is in development and is marked so.

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Areas | BP-CI-GOVERNMENT and BP-CI-CORPORATE: network operations, asset maintenance, customer and community service, emergency response, procurement or supplier management, IT, operational technology |
| Critical operations | Service supply (power, water, transport); safety systems; emergency response; the consequence question at decision 2 |
| AI reaching control systems | Authority A6 (changes systems); B3 candidates: any AI changing setpoints or switches without a person's approval; AI with a path from the internet to control systems |
| Supplier remote access | Failure case; the provider questions and evidence ladder |
| Governing body | The accountable authority for a public operator; the board for a company or concession holder |
