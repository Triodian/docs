Insurance is regulated by the states, coordinated through the NAIC. The pack `usInsurance` is built on the insurance baseline.



## The instruments

| Instrument | Tier | What it reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAIC Model Bulletin on the Use of AI Systems by Insurers (December 2023), adopted by about 25 states and DC | Expected; binding where a state has made it so | A written AI programme, governance, testing, third-party oversight; names the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 as frameworks a programme may rely on |
| NYDFS Insurance Circular Letter No. 7 (11 July 2024) | Expected (New York) | AI and external data in underwriting and pricing; vendor contract terms including audit rights and cooperation with regulators |
| Colorado regulations on algorithms and predictive models | Binding (Colorado) | Governance and risk management for covered lines |
| NAIC third-party data and model vendor framework | Anticipated | Indirect oversight of model vendors through insurers |

## Where the suite maps

| Element | App | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A written AI programme | AI Board Direction | The acceptable-use policy outline and the board's direction, tagged as policy input |
| State-by-state adoption of the bulletin | AI Risk Appetite; AI Use Assessment | A pack setting |
| Vendor audit and cooperation terms | AI Use Assessment | Circular Letter 7 terms as a document-list item; evidence rung E4 audit rights |
| Fairness and unfair discrimination testing | AI Use Assessment | Classification; the insurance failure cases on pricing and claims |
