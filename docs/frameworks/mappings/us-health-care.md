HHS (OCR, ASTP/ONC, CMS), the FDA and state health and medical boards govern AI in care and coverage. The pack `usHealth` is built on the health care baseline.


> **Status.** US support is being completed: the five sector packs are built, and the worked examples and the installer's US option follow. See [United States](/concepts/united-states).

## The instruments

| Instrument | Tier | What it reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45 CFR 92.210 (Section 1557), patient care decision support tools (from May 2025) | Binding | Covered entities identify tools that use protected characteristics and mitigate discrimination risk |
| HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules | Binding | Business associate arrangements; safeguards |
| HTI-5 final rule (28 October 2026) | Binding (narrowing) | Removes the HTI-1 model-card and risk-management criteria for certified health IT from 28 December 2026; the provider duty under 92.210 stays |
| FDA software as a medical device guidance, including predetermined change control plans | Expected | Device-regulated AI only |
| Texas TRAIGA health-care disclosure; state medical-board rules | Binding (state) | Disclosure of AI use in care |

## Where the suite maps

| Element | App | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory of patient care decision support tools with the 92.210 mitigation record | AI Use Assessment | US document list; classification records the consequence to individuals |
| Vendor information HTI-5 no longer obliges certified health IT to supply | AI Use Assessment | Asked for by contract: the provider questions and evidence ladder |
| Business associates | AI Use Assessment | Provider arrangements and the information class each sees |
| Device-regulated AI | AI Use Assessment | Recorded on the arrangement; FDA guidance at the expected tier |
| Consequence to patients | AI Risk Appetite | The consequence question at decision 2 |
