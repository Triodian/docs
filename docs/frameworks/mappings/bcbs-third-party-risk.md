The Basel Committee's *Principles for the sound management of third-party risk* (10 December 2025) supersede the 2005 Joint Forum outsourcing paper and build on the Committee's principles for operational resilience and operational risk (2021). They are the banking baseline's main source.

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Areas of a bank | BP-BANKING: retail and business lending, deposits and payments, fraud and financial crime, markets and treasury, customer service, operations, technology |
| Critical operations | Payments and settlement; customer access to deposits; credit decisioning; fraud and AML screening, used by Lite scope |
| Third-party lifecycle | The seven provider questions and the evidence ladder in AI Use Assessment |
| Failure cases for the director briefing | A credit model declining on a proxy for a protected attribute; fraud blocks at scale; a provider's model change shifting decisions; a compromised payment agent |
| Uses boards consider ruling out (B3, shown only if asked) | Final credit or closure decisions with no review; unattended payment agents above a value; customer data training an external model |

The US Treasury's *Financial Services AI Risk Management Framework* (February 2026, 230 control objectives) is used as a cross-walk target for US banking, not as a baseline source.
