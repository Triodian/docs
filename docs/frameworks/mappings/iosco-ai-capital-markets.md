Three IOSCO reports inform the securities baseline: *The use of AI and machine learning by market intermediaries and asset managers* (2021, six measures); *Artificial Intelligence in Capital Markets: Use Cases, Risks, and Challenges* (consultation report, 12 March 2025); and the *Supervisory Toolkit for AI use in Capital Markets* (final report, 25 May 2026).

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Areas | BP-SECURITIES: advice and recommendations, client communications, trading and execution, research, portfolio management, compliance and surveillance, operations |
| Critical operations | Order handling and execution; client asset safeguarding; trade surveillance |
| Records of prompts and outputs | Failure case and B3 candidate: client communications generated with no record of the prompt; the evidence register control BC3 |
| Agents acting across accounts | Authority A5; agent controls BC1 (permission scope, action log, checkpoint, stop) |
| Herding on one model | The concentration measure M10 |
