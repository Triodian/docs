Annex A of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 lists the 93 information security controls an organisation selects for its information security management system, in 4 themes: organisational, people, physical and technological.

## Where the suite maps

Triodian uses Annex A to describe its own security, not within an engagement. The [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control statement](/reference/apps/install/iso27001-annex-a) is in the triodian-install repository. For each control, it says what Triodian does for the apps it manages. It supports the security answers when a client reviews Triodian under CPS 234 or a similar standard.

Within an engagement, the apps record which information class an arrangement sees and what evidence a provider can produce. They do not assess a provider's Annex A controls.
