Annex A of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 lists the 93 information security controls an organisation selects for its information security management system, in four themes: organisational, people, physical and technological.

## Where the suite maps

Annex A is used for Triodian's own position rather than inside an engagement: the [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control statement](/reference/apps/install/iso27001-annex-a) in the triodian-install repository says, control by control, what Triodian does for the apps it administers, and sits beside the security answer pack for a client's CPS 234 or equivalent review. Inside an engagement, the apps record which information class an arrangement sees and what evidence a provider can produce; they do not assess a provider's Annex A controls.
