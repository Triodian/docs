ISO/IEC 42005:2025 is guidance on assessing the impact of an AI system on individuals, groups and society: when to assess, what dimensions to consider, and how the assessment feeds the management system.

## Where the suite maps

| Element | App | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impact on individuals from decisions about them | AI Board Direction | B1 field 8 and the automated-decision worklist (1.11), which use the standard's impact dimensions for decisions about individuals |
| Consequence to individuals | AI Use Assessment | The consequence scale C1 to C5 on each classification; harm kinds H1 to H6 at B6 in Board Direction map to C2 to C5 in connected mode |
| Baselines | All | A common source for every baseline profile, with ISO/IEC 42001 |
