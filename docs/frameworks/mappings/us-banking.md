Federal banking supervisors (OCC, Federal Reserve, FDIC, NCUA) and state regulators govern AI in banking through model risk and third-party guidance rather than an AI rule. The pack `usBanking` is built on the banking baseline.


> **Status.** The US packs are built and in review; worked examples and the installer's US option follow, and US counsel reviews the mapping before any US entity uses the apps. See [United States](/concepts/united-states).

## The instruments

| Instrument | Tier | What it reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SR 26-2 / OCC Bulletin 2026-13, Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (17 April 2026; replaced SR 11-7) | Expected | Vendor models and non-generative, non-agentic AI; generative and agentic AI placed outside its scope; states it is not enforceable; most relevant above $30 billion in assets |
| Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships (2023) | Expected | The third-party lifecycle; in effect until replaced |
| Proposed Interagency Third-Party Risk Management Guidance (11 September 2026; comments closed 16 November 2026) | Anticipated | A shorter, principles-based replacement that brings in credit unions |
| NYDFS 23 NYCRR 500 | Binding (New York) | Cybersecurity programme; third-party service provider policy |
