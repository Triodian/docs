The OECD AI Principles (adopted 2019, updated 2024) are the intergovernmental reference for trustworthy AI: inclusive growth, human-centred values, transparency, robustness and accountability. *Governing with Artificial Intelligence* (18 September 2025) surveys 200 use cases across eleven core government functions and sets out a framework for trustworthy AI in government with seven enablers.

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Accountability, transparency, robustness | Common sources for every baseline profile |
| Government functions and enablers | The government-in-general baseline (BP-GOVERNMENT) and the government-managed critical infrastructure baseline (BP-CI-GOVERNMENT): areas, critical operations, typical uses and failure cases |
| Accountable authority | Where the governing body is an agency head, a statutory authority's board or a minister's delegate, the apps say "accountable authority" wherever they would say "board" |
