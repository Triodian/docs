The Monetary Authority of Singapore's (MAS) Guidelines on AI Risk Management, issued in final form on 7 October 2026, apply to every financial institution licensed in Singapore. - Sections 3 and 4 (board oversight, risk appetite, inventory, materiality) apply from 7 October 2027.
- Sections 5 and 6 (lifecycle controls, capability) apply by 7 October 2028.

Institutions incorporated in Singapore and supervised by MAS on a consolidated basis must apply the Guidelines across their group, including overseas branches and subsidiaries. An institution in a global group may use its group framework if that framework meets the Guidelines. Local senior management remain accountable.
