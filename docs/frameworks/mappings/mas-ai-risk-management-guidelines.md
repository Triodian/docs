The Monetary Authority of Singapore's Guidelines on AI Risk Management, issued in final form on 7 October 2026, apply to every financial institution licensed in Singapore. Sections 3 and 4 (board oversight, risk appetite, inventory, materiality) apply from 7 October 2027; sections 5 and 6 (lifecycle controls, capability) by 7 October 2028. Locally incorporated institutions under MAS consolidated supervision apply them on a group basis, including overseas branches and subsidiaries; an institution in a global group may use its group framework if it meets the Guidelines, with local senior management accountable.
