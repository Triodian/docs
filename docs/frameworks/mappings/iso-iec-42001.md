ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the management system standard for AI that organisations can be certified against. Clauses 4 to 10 set out the management system: context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement. Annex A lists controls. An organisation chooses which controls apply to it in a Statement of Applicability.

The suite treats it as an adopted standard, and every baseline profile uses it.

## Where the suite maps

| ISO/IEC 42001 element | App | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A.2 AI policy | AI Board Direction | The board's direction and the acceptable-use policy outline, tagged as policy input |
| A.5 AI system impact assessment | AI Use Assessment | A classification record, tagged as impact assessment evidence |
| A.10 third parties and suppliers | AI Use Assessment | Provider findings F1 to F4 and the evidence ladder, tagged as supplier evidence |
| A.3 internal organisation (roles) | All three | The role rules and separation of duties, in the shared rules file |
| A.6 AI system life cycle; A.8 information for interested parties | AI Use Assessment; AI Board Direction | Versions and review dates; the director briefing and outputs |
| Clause 9.3 management review | All three | Review dates can be set to the client's management review, so one set of evidence serves the APRA response and the AI management system |
| Statement of Applicability and limits register | AI Risk Appetite | Imported: each limit maps to a function and an authority ceiling or consequence level; check K11 names a limit above the appetite or with no board position above it |
| Clause-level tags | All three | Every output's evidence line shows its clause or Annex A control. In Australia it appears next to the APRA letter bullet. Elsewhere it appears on its own, and the evidence index is called the ISO/IEC 42001 evidence index |
| Triodian's own system | Triodian | [Triodian and ISO/IEC 42001: our own position](/reference/apps/install/iso42001-position) |

The mapping is not a certification claim for any client.
