The World Health Organization's *Ethics and governance of artificial intelligence for health* (2021) and its guidance on large multi-modal models (January 2024, more than 40 recommendations under 6 principles) are the main sources for the health care baseline.

Where AI is a medical device, 2 standards also apply, as adopted by the jurisdiction's medical device regulator:

- ISO 14971 (risk management for medical devices)
- IEC 62304 (software life cycle for medical devices).

## Where the suite maps

| Element | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Areas | BP-HEALTH: clinical care, diagnostics, patient communication, scheduling and administration, clinical documentation, coverage and prior authorisation, research |
| Critical operations | Patient treatment; medication; emergency care; coverage decisions; the consequence question at decision 2 in AI Risk Appetite |
| Autonomy and safety | Failure cases: automation bias; algorithmic coverage denial with no clinical review; patient data reaching an outside model; a model performing worse for an untrained-for population |
| B3 candidates | Denial of care or coverage with no clinician's review; patient-facing advice with no clinical oversight; health information in tools outside the organisation's contract |
| Device-regulated AI | Recorded on the arrangement; the device regulator's instruments come from the jurisdiction's overlay (for the US, FDA SaMD guidance) |
