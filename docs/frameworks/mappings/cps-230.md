Prudential Standard CPS 230 is APRA's standard on operational risk, business continuity and service provider management. The targeted amendments took effect on 1 July 2026 and renumbered the service provider paragraphs; the suite uses the amended numbering throughout.

## What it asks, as the suite uses it

| Paragraph (amended) | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| 47(c) | Policy covering material fourth parties |
| 52(b) | Due diligence on location and concentration |
| 53 | Minimum terms of a formal agreement with a material service provider; 53(b) audit access, 53(d) notice of sub-contracting |
| 54 | APRA's access to the provider |
| 55(d) | Orderly exit |
| 60(b) | Notice of offshoring |
| Critical operations | Identified, with tolerances and credible fallbacks |

## Where the suite maps

| CPS 230 element | App | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical operations | AI Use Assessment | Lite scope: the register scoped to the 10 to 15 arrangements behind critical operations; the super pack's C4 wording |
| 47(c), 52(b), 53, 54, 60(b) | AI Use Assessment | The seven provider questions; evidence rungs E3 to E5 against the named contract gaps; findings F3 and F4 for what cannot be reached |
| Concentration | AI Risk Appetite | Measure M10 (super and MAS packs); decision 4 interim position |
| 55(d) exit | AI Risk Appetite; AI Use Assessment | Substitution and exit prompts on decision 4 and the provider findings |
| Paragraph tags | All three | Every output's evidence line carries its CPS 230 paragraph beside the APRA letter bullet |

Older Triodian material citing "53(b)" for location or "para 54" for a separate relationship used the 2023 numbering and has been updated.
