*A Director's Guide to AI Governance*, version 2 (June 2026), by the Australian Institute of Company Directors and the UTS Human Technology Institute, is the guide Australian directors are most likely to have read. It sets out what a board should ask and oversee on AI, arranged around strategy, governance structure, practices and enablers.

## What it asks

Boards to understand where AI is used and what it does; to set direction and appetite rather than leave AI to management; to assign accountability across the AI lifecycle; to see that practices (inventory, risk assessment, human oversight, monitoring) exist; and to equip themselves and the organisation (literacy, skills, reporting).

## Where the suite maps

AI Board Direction was written against the guide: every step B0 to B9 is mapped to it in the app's framework content, and the director briefing at B0 is the literacy step the guide asks for.

| Guide area | AI Board Direction steps | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy | B2 goals and posture by area; B3 red lines | AI risk appetite statement, section 1 |
| Governance structure | B4 reserved decisions and the fast path; B8 accountability | Statement; acceptable-use policy outline |
| Practices | B1 inventory; B5 confidential information; B6 harm; B7 reporting and measures | Inventory CSV; 90-day action list; measures in the board view |
| Enablers | B0 set-up, briefing and pre-read; B9 resolution and next review | Director education record; board minute |

This level of mapping is drawn from a summary of the guide; the step-by-step mapping in the app checks against the guide's own headings and is part of the legal review.
