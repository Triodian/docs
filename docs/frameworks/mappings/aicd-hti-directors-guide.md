*A Director's Guide to AI Governance*, version 2 (June 2026), was written by the Australian Institute of Company Directors (AICD) and the Human Technology Institute (HTI) at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS). It is the guide Australian directors are most likely to have read. It sets out what a board should ask and oversee on AI, arranged around strategy, governance structure, practices and enablers.

## What it asks

It asks boards to:

- understand where AI is used and what it does
- set direction and appetite, instead of leaving AI to management
- assign accountability across the AI lifecycle
- check that practices such as an inventory, risk assessment, human oversight and monitoring are in place
- build the knowledge, skills and reporting the board and the organisation need.

## Where the suite maps

AI Board Direction was written against the guide: every step B0 to B9 is mapped to it in the app's framework content, and the director briefing at B0 is the literacy step the guide asks for.

| Guide area | AI Board Direction steps | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy | B2 goals and posture by area; B3 red lines | AI risk appetite statement, section 1 |
| Governance structure | B4 reserved decisions and the fast path; B8 accountability | Statement; acceptable-use policy outline |
| Practices | B1 inventory; B5 confidential information; B6 harm; B7 reporting and measures | Inventory CSV; 90-day action list; measures in the board view |
| Enablers | B0 set-up, briefing and pre-read; B9 resolution and next review | Director education record; board minute |

The step-by-step mapping is in the app's framework content and is shown beside each step.
