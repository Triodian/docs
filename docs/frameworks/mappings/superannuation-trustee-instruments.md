The super trustee pack draws on the instruments that bind or guide an RSE licensee in Australia, beyond CPS 230 and the APRA letter: the SIS Act covenants, APRA's SPS 515 and SPS 220, the design and distribution obligations, and ASIC's guidance and reports on claims handling and complaints. Superannuation has no international baseline, so this overlay carries the whole sector picture.

## The instruments

| Instrument | What it asks, in short |
| --- | --- |
| SIS Act s 52(2) covenants, including best financial interests; s 52(7) and (8A) on insurance; s 101 on complaints | The trustee acts in members' best financial interests, including when AI acts for it; insurance offered and claims handled in members' interests; complaints handled |
| SPS 515 Strategic Planning and Member Outcomes | Business planning and expenditure tested against member outcomes |
| SPS 220 Risk Management | A risk management framework the board owns, with a risk appetite statement |
| Corporations Act ss 994B to 994E (DDO; MySuper excluded) | Target market determinations and distribution conditions, including where AI distributes or recommends |
| ASIC RG 271 systemic issues | Complaints handling identifying systemic issues, including those an AI tool causes |
| ASIC REP 831 (June 2026) and REP 844 (November 2026) | ASIC's findings on claims handling and complaints oversight |
