This section sets out the full method each app follows. For each framework, it covers the question it answers, who decides, who records, the steps or decisions, the scales, the checks and the outputs.

The apps are built from these frameworks. The wording of every step comes from the framework text. Every rule about who may do what comes from the roles each framework defines.

| Framework | Answers | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Board Direction | Where does the board want AI used, what will it not accept, and what must it see and decide itself? | [AI Board Direction framework](./ai-board-direction.md) |
| AI Risk Appetite | How much authority will the entity allow AI to hold, in its own hands and in its providers', and what evidence does it expect at each level? | [AI Risk Appetite framework](./ai-risk-appetite.md) |
| AI Use Assessment | At each boundary in the chain, what evidence of AI behaviour can be produced, measured against the board's appetite? | [AI Use Assessment framework](./ai-use-assessment.md) |
| Confidentiality Exposure Assessment | For each channel by which matter information can reach an external model, what can the organisation show about whether it left? | [Confidentiality Exposure Assessment framework](./confidentiality-exposure-assessment.md) |
| Obligations Atlas | Which obligations apply to this organisation's use of AI, and which engagements suit it? | [Obligations Atlas method](./obligations-atlas-method.md) |

All the frameworks use the same 3 scales:

- authority, A1 to A6
- consequence, C1 to C5
- evidence, E0 to E6.

They also use the same 3 boundaries, B1 to B3, and follow the same rule:

- The organisation owns its positions.
- Counsel owns the legal determinations.
- Triodian provides the structure, records and checks. It does not say what any position should be.

The [Mappings](./mappings/index.md) section lists the external laws, standards and guidance the apps are built against. For each one, it shows how it maps to these frameworks.
