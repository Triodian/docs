Concept pages explain what each part of the suite is, why the suite has it and what it is not. They do not include steps. Each concept page links to the guides and reference pages that use it.

- Roles and separation of duties: who proposes, who records, who reviews; the dual-role setting; the independent reviewer
- Modes: standalone and regulated; what connected mode adds
- [Baselines, overlays and the 4 controls](./baselines-overlays-and-controls.md): sector packs, the 8 baseline profiles, BC1 to BC4, and how a jurisdiction is added
- [United States](./united-states.md): the 5 US sectors, their overlays, worked examples and what differs in the apps
- The regulated chain: directions, K11, the calibration set, register extract v2, the board view
- Evidence: the APRA letter mapping, CPS 230 and ISO/IEC 42001 tags
- Versions, drafts and resolutions: when outputs stop being drafts
- Security model: Access, one sign-in, service bindings, no secrets in repositories; data held and where
- Glossary


> We are still writing the other concept pages. Until they are ready, the [Frameworks](/frameworks) section sets out the full method, and the README in each repository on [GitHub](https://github.com/Triodian) has the reference details.
